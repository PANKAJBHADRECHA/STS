How the voice path works

The browser sends PCM audio to a local MLX model. The model uses an audio encoder and generates interleaved text and audio tokens, which its own decoder turns into speech. There is no separate transcription/chat/TTS pipeline. Audio is processed in temporary files that are deleted after each reply. Model text is the assistant’s generated response, not a transcript of your voice.
